Updated End-to-End Flow (for collaborators)

  1. 1

    Request Intake (Email Screening)

    Requestor sends initial details from an official email (@chulahospital.org or @chula.md) to napalai.poo@dd-kcmh.com.

  2. 2

    Initial Response (within 3 business days)

    BI team confirms feasibility and sends next steps or required documents.

  3. 3

    Discovery & Requirement Clarification

    Joint session to define business objective, KPI definitions, audience, report frequency, and data boundaries.

  4. 4

    Project Evaluation & Prioritization

    We evaluate scope against service criteria and queue based on organizational impact, data readiness, and reporting frequency.

  5. 5

    Data Mapping & Model Preparation

    Identify source systems, define transformation logic, and prepare governed datasets (non-real-time).

  6. 6

    Dashboard Build (Draft Version)

    Build draft with charts, filters, drill-down views, and layout aligned to decision workflow.

  7. 7

    Validation / UAT

    Requestor and Data Champion review numbers and logic against existing references before sign-off.

  8. 8

    Publish to Tableau Server

    Approved dashboard is released with role-based permissions and access controls.

  9. 9

    Training & Continuous Improvement

    Team onboarding, usage monitoring, feedback collection, and iterative enhancement.

What We Need from Users

  • A clear operational problem or management question.
  • Example reports/manual files (if available).
  • A designated Data Champion from the requesting unit.
  • Active participation during UAT and KPI confirmation.
  • Timely clarifications when business logic is ambiguous.

What Users Can Expect from Us

  • Transparent, structured BI workflow from intake to publish.
  • Support in translating KPI into robust data logic.
  • Clear communication about data limitations or gaps.
  • A reviewable draft before production release.
  • Onboarding/training before go-live.

Service Criteria (Summary)

Eligible Project Characteristics

  • Aligned with management/operational improvement goals.
  • Not intended primarily for ad-hoc personal analysis.
  • Uses periodic refreshed data (monthly/quarterly/biannual/yearly).
  • Does not require real-time data serving.

Prioritization Logic

  • Impact level: hospital-wide > department > team-level.
  • Data readiness and requirement clarity.
  • Availability of a unit-level Data Champion.
  • Usage frequency in operational decision cycles.

BI capacity is limited. Queue scheduling is governed by transparent criteria to maximize value to the hospital.

Suggested Website / Demo Wording

Your team brings operational context and KPI goals. Our BI team translates those goals into governed data logic, designs the dashboard, and validates outcomes with you before release to Tableau Server.
